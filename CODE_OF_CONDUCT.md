# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want this project to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or nationality.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, spam, or publishing others' private information.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders.

Report problems by emailing **trignod.ai@gmail.com**.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
